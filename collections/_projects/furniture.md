Through developing specialised [software tools](/projects/auttoman.html) that test structural knitting techniques, we are experimenting with new, modular forms for furniture, knitted using industrial machines.

This work builds on prototypes developed by B in partnership with [Coyle Studio](https://coylestudio.co.uk/) and furniture designer [Eleanor Murphy](https://designeverything.org/people/el-murphy/).
